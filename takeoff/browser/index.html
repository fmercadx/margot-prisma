Click one symbol. Count every copy.

Open an electrical drawing set exported from CAD, click one outlet of each kind, and get a count for every sheet, with each copy marked on the drawing.

or drop the PDF here

  1. Pick a sheet, type a name like Duplex receptacle, and click one on the drawing.
  2. Add one example of each kind you want counted.
  3. Press Count. Green, blue and other colours are counted; orange needs your eye.
  4. Click any box to count or drop it, then download the spreadsheet.

The drawing is read here, in your browser. It is never uploaded anywhere. The first visit downloads about 30 MB of tools; after that it starts quickly. Scanned drawings can't be counted: the PDF has to come from CAD.

Drag to scroll. Zoom in to click small symbols.
