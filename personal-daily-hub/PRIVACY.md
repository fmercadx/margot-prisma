# Privacy Policy — Personal Daily Hub

**Developer:** Quiet Hearth Software
**Effective:** November 26, 2026
**Contact:** CONTACT_EMAIL

Personal Daily Hub is a daily planner and log: focus, notes and to-dos,
habits, weekly goals, water, sleep, energy, mood, expenses, a journal and
countdowns. This policy explains what happens to what you enter.

## The short version

Everything you enter stays on your phone, encrypted. We don't collect it,
we can't see it, and the app never sends it anywhere. There are no
accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## What the app stores, and where

Everything you type or log in the app is stored **only on your device**,
in the app's private storage. Every saved value is encrypted with
AES-256-GCM. The encryption key is created on your phone the first time
you open the app and is kept in the phone's secure key store (the Android
Keystore or the iOS Keychain). It never leaves the device.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** The app does not send your data to us or to anyone else. It
has no account sign-up, no server, no analytics or crash-reporting
service, no advertising and no third-party tracking code. The Android
version is built without permission to use the internet.

Because nothing is collected, nothing is sold, shared, or used for
advertising or profiling.

## Notifications

If you turn them on, the app schedules reminders on your phone: a daily
reminder, water reminders, a wind-down reminder, and the end of a focus
session. They are created and delivered by your phone's own notification
system; no push service is involved. Their wording is fixed (for example,
"Time for a glass of water") and never includes anything you have logged,
since a lock screen can be seen by others.

## Permissions

- **Notifications** — only for the reminders above, and only if you turn
  them on. Android also uses a "start at boot" permission so reminders
  survive a restart.
- **Fingerprint, face or device PIN** — only if you turn on App lock. The
  check is done by your phone; the app never receives your fingerprint,
  face data or PIN.
- **Vibration** — for the light feedback when you tick or save something,
  which you can switch off in Settings.

## Backups and exports

- **No automatic cloud backup.** The app opts out of Android's automatic
  backup, so your data is not copied to a Google backup.
- **Export is your choice.** If you export your data (or your expenses as
  a spreadsheet, or share a day as text), the app hands the file or text
  to your phone's share sheet and you decide where it goes. An export is
  plain text unless you protect it with a passphrase. Once you send it
  somewhere, that app or service's own privacy policy applies.
- **Restore** reads a backup file you pick, on the phone. Nothing is
  uploaded.

## Deleting your data

You can delete everything at any time in **Settings → Erase all data**.
This removes every stored value and then destroys the encryption key, so
even an earlier copy of the app's storage can no longer be read.
Uninstalling the app also deletes its data from the phone. Since we hold
no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

## Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.
It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Google Play and Apple

When you download the app, Google Play or the App Store processes that
download under their own privacy policies. Google Play may also run its
own checks when the app is installed or opened, for example to confirm it
came from Google Play; those are covered by Google's privacy policy, not
this one. If the app ever offers paid features, payment is handled
entirely by Google Play or the App Store; we never see your card details.
