Personal Daily Hub puts your whole day on one calm, dark screen, and keeps it on your phone.

Focus
• Today's focus: one line for what matters most, with a focus timer (15 to 60 minutes) that notifies you when it ends, even if the app is closed.
• This week: up to three goals for the week, and a short look back at last week.

Capture
• Quick notes and to-dos. Bring unfinished to-dos over to today.
• Pin up to five notes to the top of every day.
• Search everything you've written.

Habits
• Four built-in habits, or create your own.
• Choose which days each habit is for, skip a day without breaking the streak, and see each habit's last four weeks.

Wellbeing
• Water, sleep, energy and a five-face mood check-in.
• Optional water reminders and an evening wind-down reminder.

Money
• Log expenses by category, with daily, monthly and per-category budgets.
• Monthly bills that add themselves, and a button that hides every amount.

Reflect
• A short daily journal with a different question each weekday, plus three good things.
• On this day: what you wrote a week, a month and a year ago.
• History charts, a month calendar shaded by habits or by mood, and insights from your own logs.
• Countdowns to the dates you're looking forward to.

Private by design
• Everything stays on your phone, encrypted with AES-256. No account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics.
• The app has no permission to use the internet.
• Optional app lock with your fingerprint, face or PIN.
• Export a backup (optionally protected with a passphrase), restore it, or erase everything.

Make it yours: show, hide and reorder the cards on your home screen.
