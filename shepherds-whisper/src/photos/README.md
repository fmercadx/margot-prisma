# Artwork

This folder holds one file per image slot on the page. Two kinds live here.

**`.svg` — the illustrations the site ships with.** These were drawn for this
site in its own palette. They are original work, so there is no licence to
track and no stock library to credit, and an illustration does not claim to be
a photograph of a particular room.

**A photograph you add — it wins.** Drop a file named after the slot and it
replaces the illustration automatically. Raster formats override `.svg`, so you
do not need to delete anything; add `garden.jpg` and the garden illustration
steps aside. `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.webp` and `.avif` all work.

Alt text adapts on its own: while a slot holds an illustration it is announced
as one, and a real photograph is announced by its caption alone.

## Shooting the real ones

Replace these with photographs of the actual house before the site is doing
real work for you. A family who sees a room here and a different room on the
tour notices, and captioning someone else's interior as your own is the kind of
claim the FTC treats as deceptive advertising.

Shoot wide, in daylight, with the lamps on as well. Aim for the long edge to be
around 2000px — the build fingerprints and compresses from there. Match the
slot's shape roughly: `hero-living-room` is portrait, `private-room` and
`living-room` are landscape, the rest are 4:3.

**Get written consent from a resident or their legal representative before
publishing any photograph that shows them.** A resident's image, and the fact
of their residence, are protected health information.
