Privacy policy

The short version

Credit reports are read and analysed on your own device and are never sent anywhere. There is no server that receives them, so there is no copy of your credit file for us to keep, lose, sell or be compelled to hand over.

The only information that ever reaches us is a subscriber's email address and licence key, from the mobile app, so we can tell whether a subscription is active. The website collects nothing at all.

Who this covers

This policy applies to the Credit Analyzer website at analyzer url and to the Credit Analyzer mobile apps for iOS and Android. They behave differently and are described separately below.

The website

The website collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels, and no cookies set by us.

When you load a credit report into the page, the file is opened by your own browser. Parsing and analysis run inside the browser tab as WebAssembly. The report is never uploaded, and the reports and findings are discarded when you close the tab. The build makes no requests to any other domain — the entire runtime is served from this site rather than from a content delivery network, specifically so that nobody else learns who uses this tool or when.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub's servers receive the requests your browser makes, which includes your IP address and browser type. We do not have access to those logs and cannot connect them to anything you analysed. GitHub's handling of that data is governed by its own privacy statement.

The mobile apps

What stays on your device

All of it, except the licence check:

  • Credit reports you open, and every analysis produced from them
  • Reports you save, which remain on the device until you delete them — the delete action removes the files, not only the list entry
  • The professional attestation you complete at first launch, including your licence number and company

None of that is transmitted to us. The analysis works with no network connection at all.

What reaches us

Your email address Identifies your subscription at sign-in While the subscription exists

That is the complete list. We do not receive your borrowers' names, their credit reports, the findings, or anything about the files you review.

Who else is involved

  • Stripe processes subscription payments. Card details go to Stripe and never to us.
  • Cloudflare hosts the licence verification service.
  • Apple and Google distribute the apps and may collect their own information about downloads under their own policies.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. There are no advertising or analytics services in the website or the apps.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to privacy contact address.

Deleting your data means removing your subscriber record: your email, licence key and payment identifiers. It is the only thing we hold. Cancelling your subscription and asking us to delete the record removes it, and we will confirm when it is done. Deletion ends access to the apps, because the licence check is what grants it.

Anything on your own device is under your control. Deleting a saved report in the app removes it, and uninstalling the app removes the rest. We cannot delete those for you because we cannot reach them.

Depending on where you live you may have further rights under state or national privacy law, including the right to complain to a regulator. Write to the same address and we will help.

Security

The licence check travels over HTTPS and the app refuses to use an unencrypted address. Subscriber records are held in access-controlled storage. Because we never receive credit reports, the most sensitive material the product touches is never in our custody at all — which is a design decision rather than a policy promise, and it is the reason the rest of this page is short.

Children

This is a professional tool for people who originate or broker mortgage loans. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the effective date above, and for anything significant we will tell subscribers by email before it takes effect.
